Are You Compliant with the 2020 Regulations?

The Environment Agency introduced new General Binding Rules affecting properties with off-mains drainage. If your septic tank discharges directly into a watercourse, you must upgrade your system.

Our experts offer free compliance checks and can advise on upgrading to a BS EN 12566 certified Sewage Treatment Plant.
